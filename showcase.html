The stack

Models, and why each earned its slot

Every model is commercial-use safe. The generator was chosen for quality first; speed was bought back with distillation rather than by compromising the look.

RoleModelWhy this one
Avatar generation Wan2.2-S2V-14BChosen Best full-generation quality — the whole head rendered coherently. Apache-2.0, safe everywhere.
Speed Wan2.1-T2V-14B distill LoRA 4-step inference (vs 40). S2V shares the 2.1 dense backbone, so the LoRA drops straight in — same model, ~4× faster.
Voice — A ElevenLabs v3 (IVC) Expressive, tag-driven delivery from a clone of the host's own voice.
Voice — B Fish Audio s2.1-pro The A/B challenger — switchable per request to compare naturalness.
Audio director Groq · gpt-oss-120b Fast, cheap LLM that inserts emotion/pacing tags without changing a word of the script.
Face & frames InsightFace buffalo_l Best-frame selection at onboarding + face-aware cropping for reels.
Studio reference Gemini 2.5 Flash Image Identity-preserving re-staging of one frame into a clean studio portrait.

Due diligence

What we evaluated and rejected

The interesting part isn't what we picked — it's what we ruled out, and why.

Rejected · quality

EchoMimicV2

The first attempt. Identity morphing and waxy faces — didn't clear the bar.

Rejected · quality

MuseTalk / LatentSync

Fast, but they only re-paint the mouth — a visible step down from full generation.

Rejected · wrong tool

daVinci-MagiHuman / LTX-Video

Fast, but they generate their own audio — can't speak the host's cloned voice.

Rejected · license + speed

HunyuanVideo-Avatar

Same heavy-model slowness as Wan, and its license excludes the EU / UK / South Korea — a blocker for a global product.

Approach

The decisions that shaped it

Quality first, speed second

Pick the best-looking model, then claw back speed with a distillation LoRA — never trade away the look.

Decouple the slow part

Video gen runs as an async job so the API stays instant; the client polls and streams the result.

Reference Studio

Users won't record perfect video — so we reimagine one good frame into a clean studio portrait. Any input works.

Format-native output

Per-aspect references + face-aware cropping give proper 9:16 reels, not a zoomed-in landscape crop.

Provider-agnostic TTS

ElevenLabs and Fish Audio behind one switch — A/B voices without touching the pipeline.

Serverless, scale-to-zero

GPU spins up on demand and back down to zero — you pay for seconds of render, not idle machines.

Infrastructure

Runs entirely on Modal

No servers to manage. One deploy wires up the GPU workers, web endpoints, storage and secrets.
